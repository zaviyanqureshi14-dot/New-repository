01 / BASICS

Computer Basics

Understand the machine that powers our digital lives.

Explore Computer Basics +

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it, stores information and produces results.

Main Components

  • CPU: processes instructions.
  • RAM: temporarily holds working data.
  • Storage: keeps files and programs.
  • Keyboard and mouse: input devices.

Advantages

Speed, accuracy, data storage and easy access to information.

Limitations

Computers need electricity, maintenance and appropriate instructions.

02 / MAINTENANCE

Computer Care

Learn how to keep your computer safe and working well.

Open Care Guide +

Proper computer care can improve reliability and help equipment last longer.

Best Practices

  • Keep drinks away from electronic devices.
  • Keep vents clear and the workspace clean.
  • Install trusted software updates.
  • Back up important files regularly.
  • Shut down your computer properly.

Why It Matters

Regular care helps prevent overheating, data loss and avoidable technical problems.

  • Schools: lessons and online research.

04 / CONNECTIVITY

Social Media

Explore how social platforms connect people.

See Pros & Cons +

Social media lets people communicate, share ideas, learn skills and discover news and creative content.

Advantages

  • Quick communication across distances.
  • Educational resources and communities.
  • Creative expression and business promotion.

Disadvantages

  • Misinformation and online scams.
  • Privacy risks and cyberbullying.
  • Distraction and excessive screen time.

Think before sharing, check information and protect personal details.

05 / INTELLIGENCE

Artificial Intelligence

Meet the technology transforming the modern world.

Explore AI +

Artificial Intelligence (AI) allows computer systems to perform tasks such as recognizing patterns, understanding language and generating content.

Common Applications

  • Chatbots and virtual assistants.
  • Language translation.
  • Image recognition and creative tools.
  • Research and data analysis.

Benefits and Challenges

AI can save time and assist learning, but it can also produce incorrect answers or reflect biases. Human checking is important.

06 / DIGITAL SAFETY

Cybersecurity

Protect your devices, accounts and information.

Open Safety Center +

Cybersecurity means protecting devices, networks and information against digital threats.

Stay Protected

  • Use strong, unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
  • Never open suspicious links or files.
  • Update software regularly.
  • Keep backups of important documents.

Remember

Never share passwords or verification codes with strangers. Contact a trusted adult or service provider if an account is compromised.

07 / CONNECTED WORLD

Internet & Networking

Discover how devices communicate worldwide.

Explore the Internet +

The internet is a global network connecting computers, servers, phones and other devices.

What Can We Do?

  • Send messages and make video calls.
  • Attend online classes.
  • Find information and use digital services.
  • Share files and collaborate remotely.

Stay Aware

Online information may be inaccurate, and public networks can carry privacy risks. Use trusted websites and secure connections.

09 / LEARNING

Digital Education

Learn anywhere with modern educational tools.

Open Learning Hub +

Digital education uses computers, online courses, educational apps and digital libraries to support learning.

Advantages

10 / GREEN FUTURE

Green Technology

Discover technology that can support sustainability.

Explore Green Tech +

Green technology aims to reduce environmental harm and use resources more efficiently.

Examples

  • Solar and wind energy systems.
  • Energy-efficient computers.
  • Smart energy monitoring.
  • Repairing and recycling electronic devices.

Why It Matters

Electronic waste can contain harmful materials. Repairing devices, recycling responsibly and reducing unnecessary energy use can help protect the environment.
